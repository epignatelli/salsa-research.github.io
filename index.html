About Our Research

The SALSA research group focuses on developing autonomous learning systems that can scale to complex real-world problems while maintaining sustainability and responsible AI principles. Our research spans multiple areas including:

  • Scalable Learning: Developing algorithms that can efficiently learn from large-scale data and complex environments
  • Autonomous Decision Making: Creating systems that can make intelligent decisions with minimal human intervention
  • Sustainable AI: Ensuring our systems are energy-efficient and environmentally responsible
  • Real-World Impact: Applying our research to solve practical problems in various domains
